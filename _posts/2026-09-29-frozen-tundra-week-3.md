# 🏈 FTFFL — Week 3 Newsletter

## Rickey Bobby: “If You Ain’t First, You’re Last.” Apparently Those Are His Only Settings.

Last week: **83.6, dead last**. This week: **154.8, first**, with Jerry eating a **55.6-point beating**. Rickey apparently watched *Talladega Nights*, took “if you ain’t first, you’re last” as a roster-management policy, and decided second through eleventh were for people who brake before the turn. A **71.2-point rebound** later, Luther Vandross Slow Jamz has traded the broken jukebox for a stock car. Shake and bake, you magnificent pain in the ass.

Scott and Jim remain **3–0**. Richard finally wins, knocking Ashley from the undefeated table. Paul rebuilds Rome with Bijan Robinson and George Kittle doing the masonry. Thomas leaves the only reversible loss of the week on his bench. And Danny starts Kyle Pitts again, because apparently the first two newsletters were delivered to his spam folder.

For once, the weekly schedule offered very little cover: **the six highest-scoring teams all won**. No bottom-half winner sneaked out the side door. No top-half scorer got robbed. If you lost, your starting lineup belonged in the losing half. Thomas, however, had the receipt for a better one sitting right there.

## 📊 THE SCOREBOARD

### Luther Vandross Slow Jamz — 154.8 · Egbuka's Clan — 99.2

**Rickey Bobby Laps Jerry, Then Asks Him to Check the Tires**

**Jahmyr Gibbs scored 37.9**, the week's best individual performance, with three total touchdowns. **Tyler Shough added 27.4**, **Drake London 23.9**, and **Trevor Lawrence 21.6**. Those four combined for **110.8**, enough to beat Jerry without assistance from the other five starters. London caught nine passes for 194 yards; apparently the coverage was an optional donation. Luther Burden III added **16.3**, giving the Slow Jamz an actual chorus after last week's seven-man mumbling act.

Jerry got **24.9 from Joe Burrow**, **20.3 from Jared Goff**, and **29.0 combined from Bhayshul Tuten and Chuba Hubbard**. His receivers and tight end supplied just **17.0**. Justin Jefferson's **4.2** came with an ankle injury that forced him out, so that disappointment belongs in the bad-luck column, not the “why didn't you know?” column. Sam Darnold's **30.7 on the bench** was the week's biggest unused individual score, but even Jerry's best scoring combination reaches only **123.2**. Rickey still wins by 31.6. You cannot rearrange the barstools into an emergency exit. ([Jefferson injury report](https://www.vikings.com/news/justin-jefferson-ankle-injury-ruled-out-buccaneers-week-3))

Rickey left only **0.4 recoverable points** behind: Terry McLaurin's 16.7 over Burden's 16.3. **154.8 out of 155.2 available, or 99.7% efficiency.** First in points, first in margin, first in lineup efficiency. Somebody take his microphone away before he starts thanking his sponsors.

### Mighty Wieners — 132.3 · Brock of Ages — 100.3

**Shawn Finally Gets a Superflex Drink. Jim Orders the Whole Bottle.**

Jim's **Brock Purdy scored 32.0**, with four passing touchdowns, while **James Cook and Jeremiyah Love combined for 39.8**. Deshaun Watson supplied **19.8** in Dart's absence. Last week's Friday insurance pickup is now producing actual starting points, and Jim's best weekly score moves him to **3–0**. After two sixth-place scoring finishes, the Wieners finished third. The schedule jokes still have legs, but this week the team brought some of its own.

Shawn got **22.6 from Brock Bowers** and **16.0 from his Sunday pickup, the Vikings defense**. Kyler Murray returned with **11.9**, finally beating Shawn's entire **6.8-point superflex total from the first two weeks**. Progress! Unfortunately, Jonathan Taylor and Ashton Jeanty combined for only **19.0**, and Wil Lutz contributed 2.0. Shawn has now scored **90.4, 103.1, and 100.3**. Three games into the season, his team has the offensive range of a bar menu that only serves mozzarella sticks.

The quarterback news makes Jim's planning matter beyond this win: **the Giants have confirmed Jaxson Dart will miss the rest of the regular season**. Watson is already filling the vacancy. Meanwhile, Chicago's **Case Keenum threw two touchdowns and ran for another in a 27–7 win over Philadelphia**, starting with Caleb Williams sidelined by his hamstring injury. Keenum's **25.4** sat on Shawn's bench after he reacquired him Monday, when Herbert and Murray had already played Sunday. That's depth for the next decision, not a Monday button that could undo Sunday's lineup. ([Dart update](https://www.giants.com/news/jaxson-dart-to-miss-rest-of-regular-season-jameis-winston-to-start-quarterback-injury-john-harbaugh); [Bears recap](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/game-recap-case-keenum-defense-lead-bears-to-dominant-victory-over-eagles))

### 149ers — 125.2 · LiquidRice;SolidScat — 91.6

**Scott Takes the Division. Hilton Takes Another Number.**

Scott didn't need Josh Allen to perform a five-touchdown rescue this time. **Allen 20.8, Jordan Love 20.5, Kenneth Walker III 20.3, and Juwan Johnson 20.3** gave him **81.9** from four spots. Johnson caught two touchdowns and beat Hilton's tight end by **15.8** all by himself. Even a zero from the Eagles defense couldn't derail a **33.6-point win**. Scott is **3–0**, leads the league with **424.3 points**, and has beaten every other team in Red. The division tour ended with Hilton getting the commemorative boot print.

Hilton's **Christian Watson 19.1** and **Dak Prescott 18.6** supplied the resistance. Omarion Hampton managed **5.6**, Dalton Schultz **4.5**, and D'Andre Swift **9.8** in superflex. With Williams out, Hilton left new quarterback Jameis Winston's **5.9** on the bench; Swift actually scored more. Even perfect hindsight only gets Hilton to **98.3**. This week's league-low **91.6** cannot be blamed on forgetting where the good players were sitting.

Hilton's **Wednesday claim of Winston, dropping Jordan Addison**, was a reasonable attempt to replace an injured quarterback. The outlook has already changed: **the Giants announced a trade for J.J. McCarthy on Monday**, pending a physical. That doesn't establish a new starter, but it adds uncertainty to Winston's runway. Jim picked up Addison on Thursday; Addison then scored **17.5 on Jim's bench**, and Jefferson's injury makes his role worth following. No win changed hands because of that exchange this week. In a month, we may be looking at the receipt differently. ([McCarthy trade](https://www.vikings.com/news/jj-mccarthy-quarterback-trade-new-york-giants-2026-nfl-season))

### PuxxPhil Cornbread Cardmakers — 102.5 · Bareback Trailer Park Boys — 93.7

**Thomas Leaves the Winning Ticket in His Other Pants**

Phil won the week's closest game by **8.8**, thanks to **Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 29.8** and **Kirk Cousins's 23.1**. Patrick Mahomes added **17.9**. Breece Hall, TreVeyon Henderson, Brian Thomas Jr., and Hunter Henry combined for **12.7**. Four starting jobs, one lunch-special portion of points. JSN now has **67.8 over the last two weeks**, while Puka Nacua remains stuck at **9.9 through three**. Phil is 2–1 because one receiver keeps paying the other one's cover charge.

Thomas got **21.4 from Derrick Henry** and **17.3 from Javonte Williams**, then spent the rest of the evening asking where everybody went. Michael Wilson scored **20.4 on the bench**, compared with **DeVonta Smith's 9.5** and **DJ Moore's 9.7**. Wilson over Smith produces **104.6 and a 2.1-point win**. Wilson over Moore produces **104.4 and a 1.9-point win**. Either receiver seat worked. Thomas found a bar with two open stools and stood outside drinking in the rain.

Phil had his own unused escape hatch: **Harold Fannin Jr. 20.6 versus Henry's 1.0**, a **19.6-point miss**. He survived it. Thomas didn't survive his. Looking forward, Phil gets encouraging news: **Jayden Daniels is expected to practice in some capacity in London**, though Washington has not confirmed his return to game action. Thomas's QB depth took a hit, with **Baker Mayfield expected to miss at least three weeks with a dislocated thumb**. Cousins buying Phil time matters; Thomas still has Hurts and Stroud, but less insurance behind them. ([Commanders update](https://www.commanders.com/news/final-thoughts-jayden-daniels-will-practice-in-london-this-week); [Mayfield update](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/bucs-baker-mayfield-miss-least-173340763.html))

### Jed York Trailer Park MVP's — 124.9 · AJ Brown and the Out of Towners — 97.1

**Richard Gets Off the Floor and Hits Ashley with the Chair**

An **0–2 team beat a 2–0 team by 27.8**. That's an upset with paperwork. **Geno Smith's 27.6** led Richard, with **Christian McCaffrey 19.6, Kyren Williams 18.8, and Tee Higgins 18.0** providing enough support to survive Drake Maye's **6.9** and a Packers defensive zero. Harrison Mevis kicked in **16.0**, more than Maye and Malik Nabers combined. Richard finally broke 100, won a game, and relinquished his assigned seat beneath the leaking ceiling tile.

Ashley got **21.1 from Lamar Jackson**, **17.2 from Davante Adams**, and **15.6 from Bryce Young**. Young now has **75.0 through three weeks**; the superflex bargain hasn't suddenly become worthless because the rest of the room caught fire. But Stefon Diggs managed **5.3** while Zay Flowers scored **12.9 on the bench**. That **7.6-point swap** wouldn't have saved him. Ashley has spent years explaining that yes, he's a guy. This week he gets to explain how he lost to the guy we ranked twelfth.

There is a real roster concern beneath the roast: **Travis Etienne is expected to miss time with a hamstring injury, with no firm return timetable**. Ashley already has Alvin Kamara, giving him an option if the Saints redistribute work, but increased opportunity still has to turn into points. Richard, meanwhile, used this win to keep White competitive: Ashley and Paul are **2–1**, Richard and Danny **1–2**. One Sunday turned the division from Ashley's private lounge into a bar fight. ([Etienne update](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/news/611323/travis-etienne-jr-hamstring-to-miss-extended-time.php))

### When In Rome — 143.9 · Oh Shoughs! — 101.3

**Rome Rebuilt in a Day. Danny Still Waiting on the Pitts Permit.**

Paul went from **90.6 to 143.9**, a **53.3-point rebound** and the week's second-best score. **Bijan Robinson delivered 34.3**, **Bo Nix 23.8**, **George Kittle 23.2**, and **Garrett Wilson 21.7**. Moving Kittle into the starting lineup paid off: he outscored benched Mark Andrews by **19.3**. Last week's empire was wet cardboard. This week's has plumbing, electricity, and a bouncer who just threw Danny into the shrubs.

Danny's **Matthew Stafford 24.4, Ja'Marr Chase 20.3, and CeeDee Lamb 16.7** kept the score from becoming a crime scene. Kyle Pitts supplied **1.0**, while Jake Ferguson scored **8.8 on the bench**. That's now **Pitts 3.0, Ferguson 28.7 across three weeks**, a **25.7-point cumulative difference**. Danny is getting one point per week from his starting tight end. The bartender gives you more points for remembering to close your tab.

The serious loss is **De'Von Achane**, whose **1.7** came before a torn ACL; NFL Network reported Monday that he is **out for the season**. Starting him was not a mistake anyone should pretend was avoidable. Danny now needs to rebuild an RB group that produced just **7.5 combined starter points** this week, with Josh Jacobs also contributing zero from the bench. Even his best scoring lineup reaches only **116.0**, nowhere near Paul. The injury deserves sympathy. The Pitts subscription remains eligible for cancellation. ([Achane report](https://amp.nfl.com/news/dolphins-rb-devon-achane-miss-2026-season-acl-tear))

## 📈 HEATING UP / 📉 CIRCLING THE DRAIN

**Scott: three wins, three strong scores.** **166.7, 132.4, 125.2** gives him **424.3**, a **34.5-point lead** over second-place Rickey. His weekly scoring finishes are first, third, and fourth. That's a much sturdier résumé than “my opponent was also terrible.”

**Phil: two straight wins, with JSN doing the heavy lifting.** The record is improving faster than the supporting cast. **337.9 season points ranks eighth**; 2–1 is welcome, but asking JSN to keep carrying the furniture is not a complete moving plan.

**Rickey and Paul: rebounds, not guaranteed new baselines.** Rickey improved **71.2**, Paul **53.3**. Both are 2–1. Rickey has topped 150 twice in three weeks; Paul has one big score after two bottom-half finishes. Both get applause. Only one gets to pretend the fireworks are routine.

**Thomas: 154.9 → 117.8 → 93.7.** Two consecutive losses, now 1–2. Last week's defeat came despite a strong score. This week he was eleventh and left a winning substitution unused. Different problem, same depressing walk to the car.
