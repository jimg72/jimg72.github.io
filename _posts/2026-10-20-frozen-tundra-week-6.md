# 🏈 The Commish Is Overseas. The Rest of You Are Underwater.

**Richard is on a Mediterranean cruise and just posted the league's highest score.**

The commissioner took a vacation and brought back **121.6 points, a fourth straight win, and the right to be insufferable from the Mediterranean**. Eleven owners couldn't outscore a man on a boat. Maybe the problem is all the attention you're paying.

Thomas took the direct hit, losing **121.6–85.0** and his Survivor seat. Scott sailed to **6–0** while leaving Kyle Pitts's 20.5 on the bench. Jim had five different one-player routes to sinking him and somehow missed every damn lifeboat.

Shawn finally paired a win with convincing football. Jerry survived the staffing crisis. Hilton's kitchen failed its follow-up inspection. All aboard.

## 📊 THE SCOREBOARD

### Egbuka's Clan — 113.9 · LiquidScat over Rice — 97.7

**Jerry Patches the Boat. Hilton Changes the Menu.**

Jerry answered last week's bye-week staffing alarm with **Bhayshul Tuten's 22.7, Chuba Hubbard's 18.6, DK Metcalf's 14.8, Emeka Egbuka's 14.3**, and Harrison Butker's 13.0. Sam Darnold supplied 12.6. Jerry finally invited his namesake to the meeting.

Pat Freiermuth followed last week's unused 19.0 with a starting **2.1**, because fantasy football has the emotional maturity of a raccoon. Jerry still extracted **97.5%** of his available scoring. Moving Metcalf to WR and Rachaad White into superflex instead of Jakobi Meyers adds just 2.9. 

Hilton, now officially **LiquidScat over Rice**, got **21.0 from D'Andre Swift and 20.9 from Isaiah Likely**. Last week's Watson, Winston, Washington, and Likely demolition crew fell from **111.7 to 45.9**. The whole team dropped **61.9 points** from its 159.6 eruption.

Caleb Williams over Winston, Ryan Flournoy over Watson, and Omarion Hampton over Tyler Allgeier would raise Hilton to **113.0**. Still a **0.9-point loss**. Jerry reaches **3–3**; Hilton sinks to **1–5**. New name, familiar plumbing problem.

### 149ers — 103.5 · Mighty Wieners — 100.3

**Scott Leaves the Door Open. Jim Walks Into the Wall.**

**Kenneth Walker III scored 26.9**, Jordan Love 21.9, and Josh Allen 18.9. Chris Olave added 12.3 and Mike Evans 10.9. Then Scott received **one combined point from Juwan Johnson and Brandon Aubrey** and won anyway. This undefeated season now comes with complimentary roadside assistance.

The bench held **Kyle Pitts's 20.5 behind Johnson's zero**, plus Braelon Allen's 11.4 behind Tony Pollard's 7.6. Scott's ceiling was **127.8**. He used 103.5, his lowest score of the season, and escaped by **3.2**.

Jim countered with **James Cook's 22.8, Brock Purdy's 18.5, Dameon Pierce's 13.0**, and Jalen Coker's 12.7. George Pickens scored 10.3, Deshaun Watson 10.1, and Brenton Strange 6.9. The defense and kicker combined for 6.0. The selection committee needed more.

**Colston Loveland's 15.5 over Strange alone wins. Germie Bernard's 20.2 over either starting receiver alone wins.** Two legal superflex reshuffles also win. Last week's perfect lineup became this week's benched answer key.

Scott is **6–0**. Jim is **3–3 after starting 3–0**. This one arrived gift-wrapped and remained unopened.

### Brock of Ages — 117.6 · When In Rome — 86.4

**Shawn Fixes the Experiment. Rome Loses the Shipping Container.**

Last week Shawn won with the second-worst score. This week he won with the **second-best** and left **zero recoverable points** behind. Much better material for the chemical engineer's victory flask.

**Jonathan Taylor scored 21.1, Chris Boswell 18.0, Carnell Tate 16.0, and Ashton Jeanty 13.8**. Tyler Huntley and Tanner McKee supplied 27.0 together, outscoring the available quarterback alternatives. Romeo Doubs added 10.7 and Brock Bowers 9.0. A **31.2-point win** sends Shawn to **3–3**, three straight victories after three straight losses.

Paul's **Matthew Golden 19.4, Chase McLaughlin 17.0, and George Kittle 15.4** kept the lights on. Bo Nix, Bijan Robinson, and Rhamondre Stevenson combined for **14.6**. The kicker beat the starting quarterback and both running backs together. That's an entire Roman department being outperformed by one shoe.

Paul's best legal lineup reaches **96.8**, still nowhere close. His last three scores read **139.0 → 100.2 → 86.4**. The empire is **3–3**, the offense is taking on water, and Survivor spared him by just **1.4 points**. Thomas has generously volunteered to be the flotation device.

### Jed York Trailer Park MVP's — 121.6 · Bareback Trailer Park Boys — 85.0

**The Mediterranean Beats the Trailer Park by 36.6.**

**Christian McCaffrey's 27.2, Drake Maye's 24.3, and Malik Nabers's 22.5** gave Richard **74.0 from three players**. Thomas's entire lineup beat that trio by only 11.0. Richard apparently acquired an export license for ass-kickings.

Nico Collins added 9.4, Jaylen Warren 8.8, Kyren Williams 8.2, Dominic Zvada 8.0, Baltimore's defense 7.0, and Tucker Kraft 6.2. Richard finally started both running backs whose bench exploits dominated the previous issues. Naturally, **Blake Corum scored 13.0 on the bench**. The unused-running-back franchise has opened another location.

Still, only **10.8 recoverable points** remained this time, down from 36.6 last week. Improved efficiency while away: an uncomfortable performance review for his land-based operations.

Thomas got **25.0 from C.J. Stroud and 17.0 from DeVonta Smith**, but Derrick Henry supplied 5.5 and DJ Moore **0.0**. **Tre Tucker's 24.2** sat unused. That swap produces 109.2 and saves Survivor, but still loses the matchup by 12.4.

Richard reaches **4–2**. Thomas falls to **2–4**, lowest scorer and newest Survivor casualty. One trailer park is cruising. The other just received a flood notice.

### Luther Vandross Slow Jamz — 116.3 · AJ Brown and the Out of Towners — 102.9

**Rickey Changes Instruments. Ashley Keeps Hearing the Same Song.**

Last week Gibbs and Irving provided 65.7. This week Gibbs supplied no points, Irving had 5.9, and Rickey simply moved the concert to quarterback. **Trevor Lawrence 29.6 and Tyler Shough 27.5 combined for 57.1**, the week's two highest individual scores on league rosters.

Shough jumped **25.9 points** from last week's 1.6. That's a witness changing his entire testimony. Luther Burden III added 13.5, Drake London 10.2, Dallas Goedert 9.1, and Pittsburgh's defense 9.0. Rickey wins by **13.4**, improves to **4–2**, and finally breaks the alternating win-loss pattern with consecutive victories.

Ashley got **23.0 from Michael Penix Jr., 21.2 from Bryce Young, and 13.9 from Alvin Kamara**. Kamara recovered from last week's 1.6, but Kenny Gainwell supplied 2.9 and Davante Adams 5.4. The rest couldn't close the order.

Stefon Diggs over Adams and Rashid Shaheed over Zay Flowers add **8.6**, yielding **111.5**, still short. Ashley is **2–4 after starting 2–0**, with four consecutive losses. The prescription changes. The result doesn't.

### PuxxPhil Cornbread Cardmakers — 103.5 · Oh Shoughs! — 92.3

**Phil Balances the Books. Danny Discovers the Books Are Empty.**

**Jayden Daniels scored 22.5, Kirk Cousins 20.5, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba 19.4**. Kansas City's defense contributed 12.0 and Puka Nacua 11.0. Phil's running backs combined for 8.7 and Harold Fannin Jr. added 1.4. Several suspicious line items, but an **11.2-point win**.

For once, **AA Ron wasn't the missing answer**: Rodgers's 17.7 trailed both starting quarterbacks, as did Patrick Mahomes's 12.3. **Hunter Henry's 12.8**, however, beat Fannin by 11.4. The accountant corrected one recurring charge and retained another.

Danny started **Matthew Stafford 17.9, Daniel Jones 16.7, Matt Gay 12.0, CeeDee Lamb 11.5**, and Denver's defense 11.0. Xavier Worthy added 10.8. MarShawn Lloyd and Jake Ferguson combined for **3.3**, killing the momentum.

Unlike last week's four escape routes, **92.3 was Danny's absolute legal ceiling**. Every available point, tenth in scoring. Phil rises to **4–2**. Danny falls to **1–5**, four straight losses, and earns an exemption from lineup malpractice. The inventory failed inspection.
