Gentlemen. The board is cleared, the wings are gone, and somebody
still doesn't understand what superflex means. Sixteen rounds, 192 picks, and a
truly heroic amount of confidence from men who have not been right about a running
back since the Obama administration.

Every roster below has been run through the projection model — the same one that
spent all August telling me not to draft a quarterback in the first two rounds.
Scores are the **optimal starting lineup**: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, and the SUPER
flex. Your bench does not count. Your bench has never counted. Your bench is a
parking lot for men you were too proud to cut.

## 🍻 The Toasts

<div class="tundra-trophy">🥇 <strong>Mighty Wieners</strong> — 1828</div>

Look, I built the model, I ran the model, and the model says I won. That's not
bias, that's *methodology*. Brock Purdy at pick 36 as the QB1 is the kind of pick
that makes the room go quiet in the wrong way, and then he puts up 352 and nobody
says anything for four months.

Kept Pickens at 61 and Dart at 85 — both fine, neither spectacular. The real
work was rounds 3 and 4 at the turn, where taking Purdy and Jeremiyah Love
back-to-back built the whole roster.

One tight end. Colston Loveland, and that's the entire plan. If he rolls an ankle
in week 3, this paragraph ages like milk in a hot car.

<div class="tundra-trophy">🥈 <strong>PuxxPhil Cornbread Cardmakers</strong> — 1821</div>

Seven points back. **Seven.** Phil drafted **four quarterbacks** — Daniels,
Mahomes, Rodgers, and Kirk Cousins — in a league where you can start two, and
still finished second. That is either genius or a man who kept clicking the same
button and got lucky.

The actual masterstroke was keeping Jaxon Smith-Njigba at pick 86 when the board
has him eighth overall. That's a 78-slot discount on a 276-point receiver and
comfortably the best keeper decision anyone made.

Four running backs though, Phil. Four. One hamstring away from starting Emmett
Johnson in a playoff week.

<div class="tundra-trophy">🥉 <strong>Oh Shoughs!</strong> — 1803</div>

The most boring excellent draft in the room. Chase, then Lamb, then Stafford,
then Jacobs — no reaches, no cleverness, no moments where anyone said "what."
Kept De'Von Achane at 91 against a board rank of 15.

Danny did the thing where you just take good players in order and it works, which
is deeply unsatisfying for everyone else. The only soft spot is Kyle Pitts as TE1,
a sentence that has ended friendships.

## 🔥 The Roasts

<div class="tundra-trophy">💀 <strong>Egbuka's Clan</strong> — 1596, dead last by 106</div>

Jerry. Buddy. Let's walk through it.

You kept **RJ Harvey at pick 31**. The board has him 131st. That is a hundred-slot
overpay, the single worst keeper call in the league, and it happened *before the
draft even started*. You showed up already behind.

Then you took **Joe Burrow at 7**, **Jared Goff at 42**, and **Sam Darnold at 66** —
three quarterbacks in six rounds — and still ended up with the **worst QB1 in the
league** among teams that spent a first-rounder on one. T.J. Hockenson is your tight
end. Your best receiver is Justin Jefferson, who is excellent, and who is going to
spend the season watching this roster like a man watching his car get towed.

Here's the part that hurts: back in August the model ran this draft ten thousand
times and flagged **slot 7 as the worst seat in the room** — too late for the elite
quarterbacks, too early to catch the turn. Jerry drew it, then did everything in
his power to prove the math conservative.

<div class="tundra-trophy">🪦 <strong>LiquidRice;SolidScat</strong> — 1702, eleventh</div>

Hilton opened **Caleb Williams at 5 and Dak Prescott at 17**. Back-to-back
quarterbacks. In the first two rounds.

I want to be clear that this exact scenario was war-gamed. The finding was that
QB-QB openings finish *below average*, because you can only start two and the
running backs you skipped are gone by round three. Hilton looked at that
possibility and said: what if I did it anyway, but harder.

The bench is the tell — Rashee Rice kept at 116, Cam Skattebo at 92, and a starting
tight end (Isaiah Likely) drafted at pick **188**, which is less a strategy than a
shrug.

<div class="tundra-trophy">🥴 <strong>Bareback Trailer Park Boys</strong> — 1704, tenth</div>

Thomas has **Jalen Hurts**, the second-best quarterback in the league by projection,
and finished tenth. That takes work.

The work in question: Baker Mayfield at 40, C.J. Stroud at 64 — three quarterbacks
by round six — then **six wide receivers and four running backs**, which is how you
end up starting Jordan Mason in a league with a flex you can't fill.

Dalton Kincaid at 129 was genuinely nice. It's also the only nice thing on the
receipt.

## 🏆 Superlatives

**Best single player:** Josh Allen, 413 projected points, to the 149ers at pick 2 —
forty-five points clear of any other human in the league. Scott owns the best player
in the draft and finished eighth, which is the most fantasy football thing that has
ever happened.

**Most quarterbacks:** tie, four apiece — PuxxPhil (2nd) and the 149ers (8th). So
that settles nothing.

**Most points on the bench:** Jed York Trailer Park MVP's, 1,114 points of guys who
will never start. Richard drafted a very good team and then drafted a second very
good team behind it.

**Leanest roster:** When In Rome, 846 bench points. Paul put everything in the
shop window. Admirable. Fragile.

**The slot-7 curse:** called in August, delivered in September. Jerry never stood a
chance, and then made sure of it.

starting lineup — 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, plus the SUPER flex — using my projections,

Board rank is where a player sits on that list, so "reach" and "steal" mean relative
to <em>my</em> numbers, not ESPN's. Kickers and defenses are ignored entirely because
they are not real. Two drafted players (Kenny Gainwell, Cade Klubnik) aren't in the
projection pool at all and scored zero, which is probably about right.

If you disagree with your ranking, you are welcome to build your own model. I have
been doing it since July. It took three weeks and I still drafted Skyler Bell.
