# 🏈 Richard Gets the Island. Phil Gets the Comment Section.

![Richard plants the winning score on a snowy island while Scott rows away and Phil vents into Preston’s mic.]({{ '/assets/images/frozen-tundra-week-11-hero.webp' | relative_url }})

**The island has a winner. The points race has a new leader. And Phil would like a word with Preston.**

Last week Shawn took Scott’s perfect season by three tenths. This week he went right back to Monday night, this time with Phil and Jayden Daniels chasing him. Hilton was drawing up a rushing touchdown and a two-point conversion for Daniels. Shawn figured even 1% was generous. Then Daniels hit Antonio Williams twice in the final minute and Washington kicked a walk-off field goal. Great for Washington. Still no touchdown for Phil.

“I can feel my power ranking slipping. Fuck you, Preston,” Phil wrote. I regret to inform you the power rankings are below.

Richard won the loser pool and briefly made a claim on the season points headline. Hilton supplied the appropriate Lee Corso line: “Not so fast.” The math backs Hilton: **1,332.7**, ahead of Rickey by **11.7** and Richard by **16.5**. The league scored **1,318.6** this week, down **81.2** from Week 10.

## 📊 THE SCOREBOARD

### Shawn 117.6, Phil 110.3

Daniels scored 18.1. McPherson and Okonkwo combined for 18.6 on Shawn’s side. Phil’s best legal lineup was the one he started. There’s no bench receipt to dispute, which leaves Preston as the only available appeals court.

### Richard 125.7, Scott 82.6

Kelce scored 22.4 and Geno Smith added 19.6 as Richard took both the matchup and the island by 43.1. Scott is still first at 9–2. He’s also had two weeks to discover that first place still has a complaints department.

### Hilton 146.9, Rickey 102.8

Dak’s 37.3 and Jonah Coleman’s 32.9 did most of the damage in the week’s biggest blowout. Rickey got 25.8 from Gibbs and a zero from Sutton. Hilton has won three straight and now leads the league in points at 5–6. The record and the receipts are no longer on speaking terms.

### Jerry 119.0, Ashley 95.9

Burrow scored 21.4, Chase Brown and Diggs combined for 12.6, and Jerry’s pre-MNF lead got bigger. Chuba Hubbard led the Clan with 26.0. Ashley’s Monday comeback never made it out of the driveway.

### Thomas 120.4, Paul 108.9

Paul got 24.1 from Cam Ward and 17.0 from the Bears defense. Still not enough against Thomas, who had five starters at 18.0 or better. Paul has lost seven straight, which is a lot of road work for a team called When In Rome.

### Jim 114.4, Danny 74.1

Ja’Marr Chase needed 46 to beat me and scored 5.6. Tyler Bass put up 18.0, which is a nice way of saying my kicker did more for the cause than my RB2 and superflex combined. The Wieners have won three straight. Please direct all requests for a drug test to the league office.

## 🏆 WEEKLY AWARDS

**High Score, Player of the Week and Biggest Blowout:** Hilton (146.9), Dak Prescott (37.3) and Hilton over Rickey (44.1). Hilton went 11–0 against the field and left zero points on the bench. Save the receipt; he may need it at the playoff door.

**Closest Call:** Shawn over Phil by 7.3. Two straight Monday nights with Shawn as the reason somebody else can’t go to bed.

**Highest-Scoring Loser:** Phil, 110.3. **Dumpster of the Week:** Danny, 74.1. Nine straight losses, and this week the schedule is pleading not guilty: 0–11 against the field.

## 🏝️ SURVIVOR: RICHARD WINS THE ISLAND

**Survivor winner: Richard.** Only Richard and Scott were still on the island. Richard beat Scott **125.7–82.6** in the final, so Scott is the last one out. Danny’s low score has nothing to do with the island. He’s been off it since Week 1. Congrats to Richard on winning the meaningless but still worth $60 in-season tournament.
